some reflections, writings, and rough notes

if you've stumbled upon this, you follow me on Substack, i sent you this link, or you just happened to stalk my GitHub and land here. but, regardless of who you are, i'm glad you made it here, my inner world — thoughts, rambles, reflections, the mess that i am sometimes, and that i write about sometimes.

i write about things that are rather prosaic, sometimes, turning into poetry. i only recently read about a digital garden, and this is mine, at least for some time. i write sometimes, among many other things, only sometimes. and sometimes these writings are stories in layers. you'd find it in everything, and as a reader, keep in mind that these writings control the narratives from my perspective and aren't the objective truth. it's what i think-thought-feel-felt sometimes.
