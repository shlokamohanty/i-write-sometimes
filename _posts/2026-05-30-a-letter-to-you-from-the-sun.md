<div class="letter-section">
<span class="letter-to">Morning</span>
 <div class="letter-block">To the light in you,
Your eyes, dear, are so big in the mornings, glistening through the weariness. They mirror the sunlight shining through your windowpane.
</div>

<div class="letter-block">To your warm smile,
I’ll divvy up the warmth I have to give, keeping a large share just for you, even on cold winter mornings. Your laughter is the warm glow that lights the streets, ricocheting from the snow on the sidewalks.
</div>

<div class="letter-block">To your morning breath,
At dusk, I brought you some dark chocolate. Tempered over low flames, dipped into strawberries, bitter, and sweet, at once. The morning comes by, and now all that I can taste is yesterday’s sweetness.
</div>

<div class="letter-block">To your ruffled hair,
We tossed and turned. We tumbled down the hill, yet slumbered through the night. You woke up with your hair ruffled, and I put my fingers through it to set it for the day as the sun fell on your face, brushing off the wet grass. We chuckled at the end of the hill, and the day broke in.
</div>

<div class="letter-block">To your ashy elbows,
Every morning, the skin keeps changing. We put lotion on it to make the change easier and less rough. Even though the skin changes, your elbows will still feel the same each time. So when I offer my arm to you, will you please bend your elbow?
</div>

<div class="letter-block">To your night sweats,
It was that time of year when the winds reversed. Another night of thunder and lightning, and you were monsooning over. But I like the rain, so I held you tight until it ended. Now, it’s a new dawn, and the skies have become a kaleidoscope of colors and sun showers.
</div>
</div>

<div class="letter-section">
<span class="letter-to">Night</span>
<div class="letter-block">To your dry hands,
I swam through the river, love, by our house. When the sun was too bright, I saw the riverbed as the water dried up. Soon, the rain will fall, and the river will fill. Your hands were hard to hold when the waves hit, but I’d rather swim with you than stand on the empty riverbed.
</div>

<div class="letter-block">To your hypnic jerk,
You were holding onto the pole in the carriage. My love, as the train started to move, your feet shifted, and you gripped the pole tightly. Eventually, you returned to how you stood before, and you overcame the inertia.
</div>

<div class="letter-block">To the pins and needles,
Anesthesia spared you the pain. You didn’t flinch as they removed your tooth. I brought you ice cream, but you couldn’t taste it. At night, your gums tingled. Prickling more with each hour. When the sun rises, my love, you’ll taste the warmth in the cold and sweet.
</div>

<div class="letter-block">To your heavy limbs,
I nuzzled into you as your eyes slipped into the ephemera between night and day, with your legs entangled with mine. You let the weight you were carrying over from yesterday go. It landed on a mat that was soft, conforming to it, until it couldn’t sink further.
</div>
  
<div class="letter-block">To your yawn,
It’s the end of the day, so I’ll spin you a yarn. Whisk the yarn to soften it again. Unwind the threads, so you open your mouth wide in silence and let the weariness slip away. Watch you, as your eyes close, and the yarn knits into your dreams.
</div>
  
<div class="letter-block">To your clenched teeth,
The corners of your mouth ached as you tried to bite into the baguette. Yesterday, you gnawed on a rock with your teeth, leaving them weathered. But here’s some French onion soup that I also made, letting your teeth rest for the night.
</div>
</div>
