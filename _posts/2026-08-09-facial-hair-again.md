Shloka, it’s time to shave again.

![me-and-facial-hair-1](/i-write-sometimes/assets/images/facial-hair2.1.jpg)

Okay, I’ll shave my face. Same old. Same old. Hair hasn’t grown much, and I’m biting more than I can chew. Last year of ITP is coming.

![me-and-facial-hair-2](/i-write-sometimes/assets/images/facial-hair2.0.jpg)

I didn’t have time for myself. But I spend a lot more time by myself now. I’ve grown older. I can tell. If this were last year, I wouldn’t have been able to talk to myself. But I sometimes miss the Shloka without spectacles. They were dumber. Less wiser. But, they were reckless too. They were allowed to say oops more often, though.

![me-and-facial-hair-3](/i-write-sometimes/assets/images/facial-hair2.3.jpg)

Shloka has always had a fragile ego, who held words too close, especially, and especially, when the words that were called out about them were true, and they didn’t want to admit. I still have a fragile ego, whose state is determined by a working circuit, currently. If it doesn’t work, my brain jumps to the conclusion: a preview of a failed thesis. But Shloka is older and stops to think now, that this is a one-to-many assumption: that a fragile ego while learning leads to impatience, which leads to the premise of learning being defeated. Thus, a self-fulfilling prophecy of a failed thesis.

So maybe the Shloka with spectacles is better. Because they pause to think. It's a cloak of self-awareness.

So I shave my face. I now think about a friend from two years back who was arguing with me about our house. She had something else going on. I knew it, but I couldn’t talk to her or reach out to her. I would now have handled the situation differently, maybe paused with more kindness, and tried to appreciate people for their complexities and annoyances. I’ve cut all my dyed hair. It’s all au naturale like they say.

![me-and-facial-hair-4](/i-write-sometimes/assets/images/facial-hair2.4.jpg)

So, Shloka, you have pimples. It’s a culmination of five years of high pressure and high stakes. I did 159 credits in undergrad, 19.875 an average per semester + social life + research + student jobs. The ego needs the receipt of this number for validation. I need to have a little mercy on myself. I didn’t have any time for myself then. I’ll take it slow in parts, I vow. I’ve learnt to pause. 

![me-and-facial-hair-5](/i-write-sometimes/assets/images/facial-hair2.5.jpg)

Should I trim my bangs, or let the forehead of self-proclaimed wisdom show?
