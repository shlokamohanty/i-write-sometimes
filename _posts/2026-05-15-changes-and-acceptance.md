Six months ago, a year ago, well, for the most part of my life, I hated wearing dresses. I like wearing dresses now. I wear them more often than not. I used to wear lenses. Well, the doctor now told me I should wear glasses more often than lenses, and I like wearing glasses now. But I do miss wearing lenses. 

![notes](/i-write-sometimes/assets/images/water.jpg)

I'm trying to be more mature, I think. The importance of taking breaks, the importance of processing emotions. I suddenly don't feel lonely, even though I spend a lot of time alone. I feel rather comfortable.

I used to resonate with sweet, I still do, I like being and seeing things to sweetness and kindness, but the truth is I'm also simultaneously a very complex and layered person, I'm trying to accept those qualities of people and understand them really thoroughly. But, something about sweetness feels a tad bit manipulative, people pleasing, I'm trying to stray away from that unnecessarily. Nuance. I suppose.
