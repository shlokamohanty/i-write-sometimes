###  Entity to Event: From Literal, Mechanistic Materiality to Probabilistic Materiality by Johanna Drucker

- Probabilistic materiality conceives of a text as an event, rather than an entity. 
- The event is the entire system of reader, aesthetic object and interpretation – but in that set of relations, the ‘text’ is constituted a new each time. 
- Cicero's role in the imagery of material means of communication is connected to his concept of memory as inscription on a wax tablet.

It's a dense paper. I don't quite get it yet.

- I get it, I think. A little meta for me. Text doesn't necessarily have physical meaning until someone reads it. Reading is that one-time event that makes it possible, but we can't determine the entire meaning.
- text as a form of a live performance, rather than a stored object. (doesn't a keyboard make a letter a stored object though)
- interpretation of text is like a probability distribution. a guaranteed meaning is mechanistic
- Gestalt's principles are strong probabilities of perception, not fixed
