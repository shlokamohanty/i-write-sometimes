Things have changed. I’m quieter than I used to be. I feel a lot more than I used to. I never want to go numb again. I don’t want to feel numb again. Let this realm persist longer. Long enough.

I met people who broke the walls I’d built over the years, and now I can finally feel. So did I rescue myself, or is it the people I met?

And now that I can feel, I miss something I don’t quite know.

I miss my favorite songs, the ones whose words I have yet to memorise.

I miss the feeling of home, but I don’t quite know what that means. When do I create a home when I go from one dream to another? How do you create a home?

I miss being held in someone’s arms. I don’t know them quite yet.

I miss conversations with someone. I haven’t even shared one.

I called myself a photographer once, but I take fewer photos than ever before because people and places have become like fleeting moments.

I suddenly long for permanence, I don’t know what kind, though. I’m used to change feeling like the only form of permanence.

I don’t long to stay in this country, the way I did before. The permanence feels implausible as an immigrant.

Can you chase your dreams and still have permanence, though? I’m a dreamer, always have been. The ephemeral reality of dreams feels real until you wake up.

Everything I do is for permanence in the future. I’m constantly chasing a dream. When do I wake up? When does the present hold permanence? When will I live the dream if I keep dreaming?
