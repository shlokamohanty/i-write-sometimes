I’m a complex human being. Messy, even, and perhaps crazy (let's say outlandish). I forgive easily, and I see where I go wrong, even my flaws. Because, oh hell, let me start somewhere. Aren’t we all figuring it out? All that this semester has done is made me realise that, time and time again. My new favorite word, ‘nuance’, I see it in myself, I see it in people around me. Complex creatures, with their own stories, only a few can see and accept that. So from now on, I’ll let people see this mess and carry it. There’s a rumble inside of me. The Sun, it burns every day. 

I made two projects. One is more thoughtful than the other. People love a healing arc, but is there a healing, looking at more positive things, that doesn’t necessarily heal? It’s the nuance that heals, maybe, or just makes a person more willing to see beyond. 

![notes](/i-write-sometimes/assets/images/spring_show.jpg)

My favorite moment today wasn’t many people interacting with my project, the only one that I cared about was my best friend, who made a core part of my childhood, and seeing them laugh, and smile, and someone who truly has seen and accepted my complexity, is what I cared about today, that’s all that mattered. Perhaps, there are a few who see you for you, accept it, as you show them, and go along, and that’s the beauty. I know the few who can see it, now, accept it, and appreciate it.
