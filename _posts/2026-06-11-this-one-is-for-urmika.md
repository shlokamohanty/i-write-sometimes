Urmika. One of my best friends from college. I’m shy, nervous, sometimes silly. She’s nervous, gigglier than me, bossy, and definitely not shy. Two kinds of opposites.

![](/i-write-sometimes/assets/images/virus.jpg)

We met on a day when I dressed up as ‘Virus’ from ‘3 Idiots’, and she dressed up as ‘Aditi’ at the birthday party of the person she was seeing at the time. I was friends with him then. She wanted to smoke with me, and I told her, 'let's do it!'. We struggled because we both had never lit a cigarette before that day. Later, she told me that it was her first cigarette ever.

We then happened to be on the same flight, and that’s when we became friends. My friend stopped seeing her, but Urmika and I continued being friends, and I got closer to her over time.

She told me yesterday that she’s been reading all of this secretly. And was saddened that I haven’t written anything about her yet. I told her I haven’t even processed the year-and-a-half timeline, that I met her. It’s true. I haven’t. A lot happened. But I’m glad I met her, and this one is for her. 

We’ve now known each other for about two and a half years. We’ve had moments where we’ve felt closer to each other and drifted apart in bits because we both moved to different places, and a lot happened in our lives. She’s in Kansas, and I’m in New York. We’re doing things that are worlds apart. I miss her laughter and how she would have loved the person that I’m becoming here.

![](/i-write-sometimes/assets/images/IMG_1736.jpg)

A rare image of me hugging back then, I was quite drunk too.

She’d always complain that I’d hate hugs and any kind of physical affection, but how that’s different here, and how physically affectionate I am. I love hugs now, and I’d immediately give her one. I never really told her what’s going on with me, what I’m feeling like, but I’d be present back then. Only if she were here now, she’d know that I talk a lot more about what’s going on with me and what I’m feeling like. My roommate Raviya is a lot like her. Except she’s much more shy. 

Urmika has changed a lot too, since moving, and I wish I was there to see that, the person she's becoming. When we talk now, it’s different from what it used to be. I used to think that we were best friends, despite not quite understanding each other. But, now, our relationship has changed, in a way where I see we both are trying to understand each other more through the distance.

Revisited so many memories and talked a lot about how we both were during the short time we were in the same city. I was telling her how disconnected I feel from who I used to be, and she reminded me of things that are still the same. The smaller details about my behaviors, I didn’t even realise she noticed those quietly. How much of all that is the same.

She's a fierce kind of friend, someone who’s been there for me, so this one is for her.
