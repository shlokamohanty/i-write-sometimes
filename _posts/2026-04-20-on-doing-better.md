I finally feel better. That's all. My color is back after ages, and I can feel that. I have come to accept that I'm a little socially awkward, that perhaps I can't converse all that well, and that's okay. I have different manners of communication, which are rather fine. I used to think that I couldn't get close to the people I work with, but, to the contrary, the people I work with are perhaps the few who know me really well in my cohort. I am starting to make connections within my program, which feel a lot more natural and free-flowing, finally, and I'm grateful for that. I'm finally being more honest with how I'm doing. I didn't realise that my 3-year relationship actually left me a lot more traumatised than I thought, and the magnitude of that spilled over into the formation of new relationships. After that 3-year relationship, it felt like it would be hard for people to accept me, how I am, so I refrained from sharing parts of myself, hid a lot about myself, and felt that when people learn more about me, they wouldn't be able to love me. But now, I'm finally at a good space. I know and have met people who are accepting and sweet, especially everyone in my cohort, my roommates, and Amisha and Jatin. I feel loved in many ways, and that's healing, it's present, it's there. I went to school for a while, today, after 2 weeks, and it felt good. Perhaps, this is in a way is rewiring connections. I feel good, after a really long time, that's all that matters today.
