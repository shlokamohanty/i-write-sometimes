7th September.

It's much simpler than all the things I had written earlier.

I liked reading that: 'the paradoxical human desire to both order and disorder the world' at the Met Cloisters.

I have noticed a lot about my habits and how I am as a person. I avoid social settings whenever I feel shy or even slight discomfort. I avoid conflicts and difficult situations, but that's all part of being human. Any kind of relationship requires people to stay, to accept another person as they are. I have learnt now that if I avoid social settings, I wouldn't be able to learn, so I should try, and stay, and learn. The floor, however overstimulating, is still a beautiful place. The one bubble where people are extremely accepting of everyone’s uniqueness and differences. The work around social navigation here is to try to be myself as authentically as I can, focus on learning, as opposed to producing. Use headphones when I need to focus or feel overwhelmed. But try to be social and go to things, while reminding myself to write, take time out for myself often, and recharge.

On Tuesday evenings, I need to grade 'Information Design' assignments.

On Thursdays, I'll do the bulk of my USL work since I have a break from ITP classes that day.

My schedule is fixed with 'Time', 'Fabricating Mechanical Automatons', 'Understanding Networks', and 'Prototyping Electronic Devices'. Well, I'm still on the waitlist for 'Prototyping Electronic Devices', and 'Computational Narratives' but I'll get in, I think.

Pratt Research on Wednesdays.

Regularly take care of myself by cooking twice a week, as much as I can, on Sundays and Thursdays.

Sundays are primarily chore days.

I see the point in discipline and organizational systems; you have to put in just some energy, and that energy is spent so it doesn't accumulate into more disorder.
