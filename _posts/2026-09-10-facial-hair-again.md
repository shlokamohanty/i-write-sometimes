So Alanna told me today that in one of my writings, I wrote a lot about hair. Didn’t quite notice the attention that I pay to hair. So I spent a portion of the day thinking about hair. Sometimes I draw faces without expressions, only hair. I draw myself, with spectacles more than hair now. Maybe because I’m done changing my hair color.

![](/i-write-sometimes/assets/images/huh2.jpg)

I started writing about my facial hair when Raviya pointed out that I have a moustache growing. She thinks I’m vulnerable. I can write my feelings, but unfortunately I can only talk about my feelings with a few. Sometimes, I don’t even know what I’m feeling. 

![](/i-write-sometimes/assets/images/huh3.jpg)

My hair is wavy, so it’s rather easy to detangle, I think. And I spent a portion of today thinking about jealousy before I started thinking about hair. And I thought to myself: jealousy is a controlling emotion — an insecurity. Then I started thinking that aren't most feelings controlling? Is grief the only emotion that’s not controlling? And I think those who understand grief get that 'it is what it is' and continue smiling.

![](/i-write-sometimes/assets/images/huh4.jpg)

I grieve, I think. It is what it is.

![](/i-write-sometimes/assets/images/huh5.jpg)

I suppose hair is the part of our appearance that we can control.
