Coming into ITP, so unsure of my identity, I think finally, I’m more certain of who I am as a person.

![](/i-write-sometimes/assets/images/me_specs.jpg)

My ITP personal statement was about being repeatedly told that I am a ‘Jack of all trades but a master of none’, but it is incomplete: ‘A jack of all trades is a master of none, but oftentimes better than a master of one.’ That’s true, and it will perhaps remain forever.

So, I’m Shloka, and I’m a silly goof. I’m enby. I’m actually a pretty slow learner. But I’m not scared of failing or falling, and I’ve done it many times in my life. ~~I still can’t call myself an artist, but I’m hoping perhaps, someday, people will look at me and see that.~~ I think I want people, someday, perhaps, to look at me and see me as an **artist** as well as an **inventor**. I’ve been rejected by people, places, and things, and have rejected people, places, and things. I’m nerdy, and I like academia. Coming into ITP, I again tried to find a specialization, but I truly just like telling stories in different mediums, and perhaps I’ll keep exploring and experimenting with them forever, and that’s okay. I remembered this week why I like telling stories, because growing up, my stories made my two best friends smile.

I create:

- for myself
    
- for people I care about deeply
    
- to merge the arts and sciences
    

I keep trying to find this intersection between the arts and the sciences, and keep circling back to the same notion. Will I ever find it? Maybe? At least, I think I’m closer to it each time.

![](/i-write-sometimes/assets/images/cycle.jpg)

This is the shape of my story.

I’m not confused. I like trying different things, and I think that is what life holds for me.

 ~~and observing. I didn’t think I was observant enough, but I realised I’d just been really quiet about my observations earlier. Sometimes I still am, but at least now I know and can say I’m really observant.~~ I have, in the past, felt like an observer in my experiences, in relationships with people, not being present in the moment but rather experiencing them from a detached perspective. After moving to New York, I became really observant, paying attention to every little detail to the point that it was hard to regulate my emotions without spiralling into anxiety, passing judgments, and acting on them instantaneously. So now, I realise I’m going to try to be as observant as I can, but simultaneously be a good observer. Do so by spending more time with my observations, reflecting on them and my experiences, and avoiding passing instant conclusions. To become a good observer, I need to be observant too.

I love people, and that’s why I love cities, because people make up cities.

It brings me immense joy to see people love each other sweetly, often in the small things, and I will make sure to keep doing so for the people in my life. Along the way, I know I will make mistakes, perhaps even hurt people without intending to, but I will learn each time, and mend my mistakes, because more than anything else, in life, there’s one title that I’m sure I am, and that’s a learner, and that’s me.

Am I my own experiment?
