In many of my personal reflections, I've noticed that I've talked about sounding different around people. I've noticed that often, it's about my cadence and my language around different people. I do it a lot to make others around me feel comfortable. Sometimes, that's why I think I can form relationships easily with anyone, because I can match people's energies. But it's a tool, rather than an identity thing. I used to be terrified of being alone in undergrad, or my brain not being occupied by work, or people. Now I actually feel immensely comfortable with being alone. Because I do have a strong sense of my identity now, as opposed to when I came into this program, figuring out my interests — who I am.

I realised it takes up a lot of my social energy in group settings. I struggle immensely figuring out when it's my turn to talk, jumping into the conversation. It takes up a lot of energy reading people. I don't know why I even try hanging out in big groups anymore.

In one-on-one or super small group settings, I thrive. It's easier to be more myself. I feel this missing feeling after I say bye to some of my friends temporarily. Especially after hanging out with them one-on-one, not in group settings. I feel sad when they leave temporarily. Maybe I'm meant to have smaller social circles. I don’t have as much energy anymore.

I feel much more regulated than I did during the last semester. I don't feel too anxious, excited, or too sad, just a regular flow of emotions. So I can actually understand a lot of these behaviours of mine.
