An intimate question, certainly. I’ve always liked the idea of what a girl does in her room, because for some reason, mainstream media shows mostly men and their experiences in a room, and even the depictions of women and non-binary people in their rooms are shown in the most stereotypical manner, which is not really relatable. Growing up mostly alone, the room became an interesting space for me. The four walls have seen a lot and will continue to do so.
