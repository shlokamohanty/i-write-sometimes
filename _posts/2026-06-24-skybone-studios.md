**Pitch 1**
*I wanted to use Xenon as a metaphor for anxiety. Xenon is a noble gas that is generally unreactive, but under immense pressure, it does end up reacting. The most stable Xenon compound is Xenon Tetrafluoride. My idea is to use long exposure photography to show Xenon reacting with Fluorine in a 1:5 ratio. Most of the screen is blank white, with a circle in the center containing Xenon and Fluorine. The circle gets smaller to show an increase in pressure. As the circle gets smaller, more reactions occur until the circle explodes to show Xenon Tetrafluoride as a crystalline solid. In the last part, moisture causes the Xenon Tetrafluoride to immediately turn back into Xenon and Fluorine, with some of the compound reverting as the ending.*

**Pitch 2**  
*I wanted to use paper chromatography as a technique for animation here. This would be a form of circular paper chromatography, where the character is Color, which spreads into different colors, struggling to hold itself together. With the decomposition, Color is devoid of color, leaving only expressions. So Color puts the different colors it decomposed into back together, returning to the color that Color once was.*

**Pitch 3**  
*I wanted to show the formation of a molecule and tap into the concept of string theory. This is experimental in that it plays a lot with music. Each subatomic particle is a string, and these strings come together; each string is a different note. As the strings come together, the sound is cacophonous, gradually resolving into a symphony.*
