I think about my visit to Walden Pond. It was my favourite day this year, I think. I looked at my hand through the water in the pond, the sun and water forming refraction caustics. I saw it on my arms. I pick up rocks using my toes. I stared at the rocks for a long period of time. I remember, when I was younger, I’d collect rocks and bring them back for my friends. I like rocks, you see. The patterns on them. Rocks are a part of this larger hyperobject that’s yet unknown to me. I refuse to say, my heart, the heart can’t feel, so my mind. But, like a piece that’s broken off, the pieces of rocks, the heart, it feels, when it breaks, when it’s nervous, when it’s in love, a tiny brain. My heart, my mind, it’s populated by people, the rocks, of a hyperobject, yet unknown, transcending space and time. I see us all connected through physics. We’re all made up of stardust after all. That’s how I understand the world.
