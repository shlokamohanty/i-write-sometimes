I remember as a kid, I would tell people that physics is the science. That everything in this world can be broken down into subatomic particles. Well, now I realised those can be broken down further into strings. But, essentially, everything in the world can be broken down to that. It’s beautiful. It’s the ‘god’ science, in my opinion. That biology, can be broken down into chemistry, and chemistry can be broken down into physics. And now I understand that people are collections of strings interacting with each other.

I was thinking about the clarity that I’ve been having recently, and that maybe I would have had it earlier if I had related it to physics concepts. Maybe I have always tried to understand life through physics, in some ways. That’s all I’ve done for so long.

The fact that art and science are just different methods of inquiry into nature - maybe I view everything through a physics lens - and, through coming into this program and my experiences, I tried to see the world through the lenses of the arts, life in metaphors, but that got overwhelming quickly, but it opened up something: my ability to feel. But, looking at things from just the arts lenses, I struggle to understand depth. Physics is the lens of depth for me. Science is what gives depth, and art gives meaning: the two combined give me the ability to feel deeply.

I think the way for me to look at the world should be through the lens of physics, and use that to understand the feelings, the complexity, the humanness. Maybe scientists see the world as I do. Turning science to poetry. There's something, mesmerising about the minds of those who understand the humanness, the feelings, the complexity, and use science to structure. Turning poetry into science.

This is the framework, looking at life as data, science, and turning it into art.
