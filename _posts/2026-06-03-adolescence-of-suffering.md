            I heard you babble about a dying youth.

                     mumbling about making meaning of your suffering

                    they said, find another, understand theirs, give life to their suffering 

     And somewhere along, you’d find your soul. 

                     and as you ramble along 

     At the dawn, you’re met with tears.

                     that these tears were meant for something

                     drowning along, it comes to the surface

     You’ll never find the adolescence of your suffering in another. 

    I heard you babble about a dying youth.

                     mumbling about grief, I wish the death of youth with dignity

                     and adolescence of suffering.
