Before even starting the paper, 'Interoception and dissociation in migraine', the diagram, fascinated me, especially the part about analgesic overuse astounded me, considering that is something I did without intending, while I was growing up. 

```
pain → take analgesic → temporary relief → rebound headache → take more analgesic → brain recalibrates around constant pain → chronic migraine
```

"Many individuals with migraine also suffer from functional neurological disorders and dissociative symptoms"

```
There's something about this cyclic behaviors. I think, everyone has cyclic behaviors, because, familiarity feels safer and change is uncomfortable and uncertain.
```


Koreki, Akihiro et al. “Interoception and dissociation in migraine: a case-control study of chronic and episodic subtypes.” _Frontiers in neurology_ vol. 16 1643260. 20 Aug. 2025, doi:10.3389/fneur.2025.1643260
