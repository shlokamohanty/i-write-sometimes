I was rereading a reflection I wrote on 15th October 2025. It was called ‘A day to remember, I guess’. It’s 13th June 2026. I had a breakup on 1st October 2025. That was the last time I spoke with my ex. I called him today because I owed him some closure. While talking to Urmika the other day, I realised he was my best friend in college, regardless of how things went down. Urmika reminded me of the things I did for him and made me realise that that’s how I show my affection to my friends as well, and that he was part of my life for three years. I admitted to him that my understanding of love was different back then. And admitted the hard truth to him that I actually wasn’t in love with him. But he was my best friend. He said he was in love with me. I told him I wasn’t because he didn’t understand or know me. He said, I never let him in. I agreed. It’s true, I didn’t. I didn’t let him in. It’s true, I didn’t want to. He asked if I wanted to be friends. I said I didn’t, but I did want to let him know that he was my best friend, for a while. That’s it. I didn’t want to accept it, but I did. It was a little heartbreaking. I felt it in my throat, and my eyes teared up when I heard that familiar voice, because I heard that almost every day for at least 2 and a half years, from the 3 years that we were together. I don’t know if he got closure, but it definitely felt like I did. I wished him well, then blocked him again. The summer is maturing me, maybe.

So in this past year, I’ve slowly confronted all of my relationships that ended up being formative in shaping some meaning in my life. I can now look back at it with more clarity and depth. I sometimes wonder, while confronting all this, how I will confront the one relationship that fucked me over so bad that everything consecutively got fucked over, and left me with crippling anxiety, from when I was a kid. But the pain of that relationship isn’t in that relationship, in itself. It’s probably more to do with my parents, perhaps. I don’t know. I’m still thinking about that - the how and if I have the bandwidth.

So, coming to terms with and accepting the gravity of my anxiety. It becomes a barrier in everything, and I really need to push past it. And I may need to start meds to manage that. It’s weird. I can feel the tightness in my chest. I can really feel it during breaks. That’s why every summer, my anxiety goes through the roof, and I enjoy all my time being taken up by things. But I need time by myself, and I need breaks, or else I have breakdowns. It’s almost a year - and what a year. If I were standing in front of me, and me, there would be something stopping in front of me, and that’s my anxiety. I’m confronting it, and I’m reaching out to myself through it, maybe. I don’t actually think there is a solution to this. It’s just not going to go one day. I’m just being more real about it and dealing with it.

I’m going to be working in two places this summer. As a researcher at Pratt, and as an intern at an animation studio. So, right now, I’m filling out my documents to work in an animation studio. So rereading that reflection, thinking about “Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse”. After this, I can call myself an animator. I was always a researcher. 

I used to think that, in some ways, people can be happy. I think oblivion is what makes people happy. I was in oblivion, for sure, thinking there was some kind of true happiness. I feel complete, though, not lonely. I thought I felt lonely, though. The ones that are self-aware, or perhaps, hyper self-aware like me, will wake up feeling miserable daily. I kind of like that, the misery of it. There’s some peace in it, that nothing will ever be enough, that the whole of life is the pursuit of some chase. It’s not the chase of the labels, inventor, or artist, or whatever, but something more than that. 

Working at an animation studio seemed like a dream for me, but now that it’s true, it’s not enough. But there’s nothing that will satiate that. Hence, I want to devote my life to research, because there’s a constant chase in that, and in a manner that I like. A professor at an arts school, with my research practice on the side. It is stupid that I thought I needed to figure myself out. I don’t. The whole of life is figuring out.

So “Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse” might not be the artwork that moved me, as I realised quickly after I wrote the reflection. That time of my life, I used to use games to translate things, haha, looking now, I would not use games to translate. Jisoo, at the Resistor, was showing me this artwork of someone who made a map of breakups in New York, saying I would like it, and the truth is, I wouldn’t make sentimental artwork like that anymore. The whole year has been a way of finding my practice. I think my practice is in the complex. I want to use arts and sciences to create things that leave people thinking, without directing them to feel something. I am a truly complex person, and a secure person for the most part in my identity now, constantly figuring things out, and on a quest for something, and I like myself. I want to leave people feeling that complexity. 

An artwork that moved me. Well, right now, I would say "Project Hail Mary". Grace is a lot like me. But I don’t know. I feel like I haven’t found an artwork that truly moved me yet.
