Sometimes I think I’m so silly. I’m having these revelations, which I think are revelations, but they are just objectively human things. When I show these to Raviya, she’s like, it’s like your first day being human. Sometimes, the answers are so clearly in front of me, and I’m just so oblivious. I love linguistics and research, and I clearly have an interest in archaeology and anthropology, as well as the sciences. Makes sense, auto-ethnography as a practice, and the fact that the paper that changed my life was the 'Politics of Transcription'. Even the decomposition as a method is archaeological. I’m so stupid. Or I’m just living life with some form of childlike wonder.

Maybe I need to have a section for rants on my blog. This is going to be a rant. Not a reflection. I was explaining to Raviya that this might be my last year in New York City. Truly, when else will I make a big move in my life if not when I'm 24? I would have lived here for 6 years by the time I graduate. With my visa expiring, I can't even travel outside the country. I'm just bound by this country. So, I should leave after graduating. Feeling frustrated and feeling out of place. While I love my program, I am still feeling out of place. This might not be the place I'm supposed to be at, or maybe the timing isn't right. I'm young, inexperienced, and slow at learning in a fast-paced environment. Made it off the waitlist. I wasn't really meant to be here, I guess. Dejected that I'm not even competitive enough for any student jobs within my program. I've been rejected so many times, by so many people and places, it's a known feeling, the feeling of heartbreak, and I'll pick myself up, because I do each time. Just a year of heartbreak after another, then another, I'm tired. Things working out feel unfamiliar. Acceptance feels unfamiliar.

I'm just frustrated. Feeling that, that momentarily, I just want to wallow in self-pity. Which isn't even going to help me. 

Now the reflection. Made it off the waitlist. Some small wins. The reality is that I will be rejected most of the time in life. Acceptance is more rare, and as it should be. You need to be accepted once for it to work out. That there will be many rejections before an acceptance. A lot of it depends on timing and place, so many, many, many factors.
