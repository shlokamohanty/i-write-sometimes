_Content Warning: This post discusses childhood abuse._

Many people in my cohort are older than I am. They have come into this program with much more experience and ambition than I. While I’m here, thinking about whether I even look at myself as a designer or an artist, or whether I will even after two years. They have big dreams and aspirations, and I love that for them. But I’m also jealous that I don’t even think about that. One person in my cohort aspired to be famous in the same way Sol LeWitt is. But these are some thoughts that could never cross my mind, not because I don’t want to do great things. I just haven’t had the space even to picture doing these.

So last night, instead of sleeping, I stayed up tossing and turning, thinking about this. As a matter of fact, today in the shower, I realized it’s tied into my story. So let me explain my story.

So here’s my story. Lately, I’ve been thinking a lot. By a lot, I mean a lot. October 1st, I knew I had to end my 3-year relationship. I had a huge presentation that day where I had to organize a game for many people, but in the back of my mind, I was thinking about this. I had to put up a happy face in front of a friend and my uncle that day, and on my way back, I broke down in the subway. My neighbor saw me crying as I entered the building. I knew what I had to do. I was up until 4 am that night, breaking up with my ex. During the breakup, I was also working on an After Effects assignment on the side. I didn’t end up presenting the After Effects assignment in class the next day (It’s not essential to the story, but I thought I’d add this fact). But after this one day, I don’t feel any sadness; in fact, I feel a sense of relief.

Now, why was this important? Because lately I’ve been thinking a lot about love. I don’t mean merely loving someone, but loving to do things. Or love in general, for that matter.

I used to attend Billabong High International School. I had a great friend group, and I loved learning there. My favorite teacher there, Dr. Jikku Jose, made me realize my love for the sciences. Things were going great. A feud within the Goenka family, who were the school's trustees, led to months of administrative issues. As a result, teachers stopped attending for months, prompting my parents to change my school.

That’s when I moved to another school, Singapore International School. This is where things took a turn. The school's overall atmosphere was quite interesting, as they presented themselves as an up-and-coming, innovative institution. However, it was surprising to find that many of the faculty members held really sexist views. The only good thing coming out of this school has been my two friends, Amisha and Jatin, who I know will be my friends for life. Additionally, I've come to understand that relationships and love can be unconditional, thanks to my friendship with them.

Coming to this school was interesting because why am I being forced to play all the sports seasonally? And why are there only two other girls in my class, one of whom is absent most of the time? Before this point, I didn’t even know what the feeling of a crush was, and I guess after being nudged for a bit into thinking about crushes, I ended up having my first crush that year, moving into the school. We ended up dating in 10th grade, for a month (really important for the story, I swear).

Fast forward to 7th grade (this is where things really take a turn). I get into my first relationship, which lasts 6 hours. HAHA! But later that year, I also got into another relationship, which was on-and-off for four years after. I hated him at the beginning. He was really disruptive. I was academically smart, and he was honestly really dumb. But people loved him, even though they got really annoyed at him. Everyone loved him, at least then. Even the teachers did. Because, for some reason, in our school, the teachers encourage the students to bring a bit of fun to the class. My parents were also really occupied at home because my brother was only two years old. This setup was the perfect setting for me to fall for this guy.

At first, we dated for a month. Things were alright until he told me after that month that he liked someone else, and that person liked him back. So, I backed out and ended it. But then, another month after that, he tells me again that he has feelings for me. After this point, the process becomes cyclic, repeating itself. Continues over the summer break. But something changes after the break.

_This part gets violent and graphic, so exercise caution when reading._

It begins with sexualising hugs. And then this anger towards me. Wounds on my face and more. Visible enough that teachers would pull me aside and tell me not to get into a fight with a boy. But here’s what, I wasn’t even the one who was being violent, and no one told him to stop hitting me. Then on my birthday (why I hate my birthdays and also why for the longest time I hated people who played football, but that changed because my brother now loves football), I get mad at him for everything, finally stand up and throw his football studs at him, and then in retaliation he grabs me by my hair and pushes me against a cupboard. More wounds. While this is happening, ten guys from my class are just watching, and no one stops. Then a guy from an older grade sees this happen from the outside and intervenes by holding him in a chokehold. I made it to my bus that day, but everything was hazy.

The next day, the guy who intervened told the coordinators at the school about the incident. They ask me if they should tell my parents. I wish I had known better then, but I had only just turned 13 and didn’t want to lose whatever this was, so I said no. What’s funny is that the school, instead of doing the right thing and telling my parents anyway, decides to make sure that this fact remains hidden. So, after this, everything returns to its original state in a cyclical pattern.

Then the following summer, things become more intense. I get coerced into sending nudes. Then, once school starts, he threatens me with r*** and exposing the nudes, if I’m not sexual with him. After this day, when I came back from school, my parents checked my phone and could tell something was off. I break down and cry, telling them everything I haven’t for years.

Then they confront him and his parents, and somehow this makes his actions worse. My parents take my phone away, and everything is restricted. They believe this is the best solution for me to resolve the situation. For a while, in school, he and I spoke no words to each other, but this is where the sexual abuse starts. I have a weird relationship with learning music theory, mainly because of this one experience. I was in a class with most of the same men who stood still and did nothing, and at the same time, I was experiencing unwanted touch within the class. Sometimes I even had to sit on the floor to avoid this. When I spoke up about it with a counselor in the school, she gaslit me into thinking this was my fault, but in reality, she wanted to cover it up.

_The less intense stuff._

So I never really stood up, never really told my parents what happened after they intervened. I slowly separated myself from the situation, became detached, and in 10th grade, I left that relationship on a sudden day.

Blocked him from everywhere, never spoke a word to him in person, even though we were in the same classes when he was in school.

Now, the sudden day that I chose to leave. This is where my first crush comes in. We were really good friends before this point. My mom liked him a lot at this point, so she would let me hang out with him late at night. We would often walk around in parks in Powai (I can feel Amisha laugh at this), sometimes lie on the grass, and talk about our lives. So this is a guy I’ve liked sort of, but not really, for a while, and created a safe enough space for me to leave that relationship. Our first date was right before a Vir Das show (you’re allowed to laugh), interrupted by Jatin, who saw us walk together, again, guess what, in Powai. By the way, don’t give any brownie points to this guy. He turned out to be a pathological liar, and Amisha and I later discovered that very same year. We broke up within a month, but that’s alright. It wasn’t that serious. The main reason for the breakup was that I was too much of an emotional burden, and that I couldn't picture a future together. What future… we’re in 10th grade…? Even after the breakup, we'd still get breakfast every weekend and talk about our lives in the same way, even in parks, until I realized he was a liar.

So, my getting out of my abusive relationship should have been this big thing. I was going to slap the person for putting me through so much. Instead, it was just silent detachment.

After that, I’ve also been with many other people. Some were better than others. A really sweet relationship, it was bound to end because we were both moving away for undergrad. Personally, that was a big heartbreak.

But even though that was a big heartbreak, some part of me has never fully loved after that abusive relationship (_except I’ve only really truly loved platonically, because through all of that, Amisha was there_). I’m not sure if I will. But I want to try to relearn how to love, even though it might seem really hard, especially romantically.

Now, growing up, I was just surviving, going through this, but also scoring well while also having really bad ADHD and Anxiety, which was only diagnosed in 11th grade. While others are figuring out their passions, I’m just trying to be good enough so my parents won’t notice all the other things going on with me. So, play the easy game, score well, and people will leave you alone. You get it.

I came into undergrad as a physics major. Though I really liked physics, I always knew I didn’t want to pursue it as a career. The way I have loved physics and will always love it is similar to how people pursue hobbies, or in conjunction with what I might want to do. But, because I’ve always been the number cruncher kid, doing a STEM subject seemed like an easy and somewhat obvious choice for me to go into that.

So, even though I started undergrad as a physics major, I explored various subjects to discover what I might enjoy, essentially what I should have been doing in school. At one point, I declared five majors and took extra summer classes. I graduated with 159 credits in 4 years, still unsure, but more sure that it is a mix of sciences and art. So yes, I did end up majoring in economics and data science, but I also pursued many other interests beyond those fields. While also learning and figuring so much out, because it’s my first time living alone, moving to a whole new country, and also having this unprocessed trauma, I never even thought it required any processing. It was a lot, and not a fun experience.

During this time, I met my most recent ex. He’s also loved by everyone, including my parents and grandparents, and is the conventionally nice man. We had been dating for three years, and it’s mostly good. Except very early on, into dating, I realized I wasn't really in love with him, but things made sense because he became one of my best friends. After the truckload of horrible relationships, someone who is just ‘nice’ and cares for me was all I wanted for a moment.

It wasn't until after moving here that I started building things daily at ITP and talking to people who were doing the same and enjoying it, that I realized there’s much more I want out of life. I want to be so full of love with people, with things, with everything I had forgotten.

That ex also cheated on me. I knew my ex was cheating on me months before, though, but even after that, I knew that he cared for me and was inherently fine, so I let it go, in that moment. But the detachment kicks in. Then, on October 1st, I realized that being cheated on is a feeling that is so inherently normalized for me, even though it's not really something that’s normalized, and the act of doing that is so much more. But, even more importantly, he was thinking of moving to the UK, and I considered that if he did, maybe I should, because that’s the future. While thinking about that, I realized there’s so much more I want to do here first that I want to keep doing what I started and continue loving it. I realized that I’m not in love with him, and if I stay in this, I probably won’t ever be in love with anyone, and I'll continue to look at a future, one where I’m not in love with anything.

So I’m finally 23, and I’m alive. I made it this far, even with three near suicide attempts. For the first time, ever since New York (Harry Styles, yes!), I’m not just surviving, I’m closer to doing things that I like in a day.

So, thinking about my goals, maybe eventually I’ll be able to dream big and set myself up for success. Regardless, for now, I want to dive deeper into whatever I’m being taught and actually relearn how to love making things, love people, and love living (cheesy).
