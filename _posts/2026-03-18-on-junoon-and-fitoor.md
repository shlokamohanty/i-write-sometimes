I spend a lot of time thinking by myself. I got called a planner yesterday, and truly, I don’t sometimes see myself as one, so that was a bit of a shocker. But then I thought that I keep making plans, backup plans, and plans for those, almost continuously. I am not a routine person, but I do have certain habits I repeat. In some ways, subconsciously, I follow a routine. Last semester, I got into the habit of picking up groceries on my walk home in the morning, about once a week, and this semester, I found myself without any groceries at home many times because the walk back home stopped. Didn’t even realise that, grocery shopping in the morning became a routine. Things like that. Perhaps I don’t give myself enough credit for the system that I create in my head.

So while I spend time thinking to myself, planning perhaps, I toy with this idea often that if things are meant to be, then they happen, or do you make them happen? Perhaps you do make them happen, but there’s some magic in the idea that if things are meant to be, then they happen. That thought always takes me back to this unanswered mystery in physics, ‘Baryon Asymmetry’. The Big Bang was supposed to create equal parts of matter and antimatter. Equal parts of matter and antimatter means complete annihilation. But somehow, there was just a tiniest bit more matter, and that is how everything exists as it does. Is life just deterministic? So maybe, if things are meant to be, then they happen, right? But what I realised recently from reading is that no one knows the universe’s initial conditions. The keyword, ‘supposed’, was supposed to create equal parts of matter and antimatter. Maybe because of the initial conditions, there was just more matter. So maybe, in some ways, you make things happen.

I think about how I’ve always wanted to do what I’m doing right now in my graduate school, it’s something I’ve perhaps always known that I liked, in some ways. I tried everything else in undergrad, only to end up here, like it’s meant to be, that this is what keeps me alive, this weird passion to keep learning, to keep creating at this intersection of things. What’s meant to be will happen, and then again, I am the one who submitted all these applications to graduate schools in a field like this, so I guess I did make it happen. It’s a little bit of both, perhaps, you do things to try and make things happen for you, and then a little bit is left up to the magic, or rather the decision of others, that’s not in your hands, the magic of rejection and acceptance, I guess.

So maybe I should keep planning, as I’ve always done, and it’s just a byproduct of this weird passion that I don’t quite understand, what drives me.

And on ‘Junoon’ and ‘Fitoor’. I love coming up with names for hypothetical kids that I might have (most likely not), and while pondering this, I thought my hypothetical kids should be named ‘Junoon’ and ‘Fitoor’, and both mean passion. The irony in the names is that these hypothetical kids might never exist because of this very _Junoon_ and _Fitoor_. My roommate said, unless the hypothetical kids become my _Junoon_ and _Fitoor_.
