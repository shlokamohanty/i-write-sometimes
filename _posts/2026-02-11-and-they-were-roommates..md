Before starting undergrad, maybe a month before coming into undergrad, I came out as bisexual on Instagram. During my life, I’ve liked girls longer than boys. My first kiss was with a girl. I’ve kissed more girls/enbys, dated around with more girls/enbys, but been in more long-term relationships with boys. So in India, I was in school when I came out, but after coming into undergrad, I got this freedom, away from parents, away from societal judgment. I could wear what I wanted, I could do what I wanted, and there wasn’t anyone stopping me. I could stay out as late as I wanted. The first taste of freedom that year never tasted better.

Before moving to Madison, the university had an Instagram page for connecting with future classmates and potential roommates. That’s where I first saw Myra. She was also from Thane, India, and we had mutual friends. We started messaging, often doing BuzzFeed quizzes instead of talking about how it would work as roommates. My boyfriend at the time thought I had a crush on her. I denied it, though I did think she was pretty.

So we decided to meet at Korum Mall and play arcade games. She was a dancer, so we did a dance-off. I wasn’t really a dancer, but I tried anyway. We clicked pretty instantly, I thought. But, because my Hindi was bad, she thought I was a little pretentious. We got a jewellery kit with the tickets we bought, and we made a beaded necklace together while eating at McDonald’s. So we decided to be roommates.

She invited me for lunch with her brother, but I was busy moving in and hanging out with a friend from school. Still, she later told me she had wanted to hang out with me that day. During the first week, we quickly decorated our room. The room was really colorful, pretty much the opposite of the kind of colors I like. Lots more pinks and greens. I like neutral colors: white, brown, and black. But because she liked that, I didn’t mind having our room decorated that way.

For the first month, a COVID-19 scare in my dorm forced me to temporarily move into my friend’s dorm for two weeks. This meant I became closer to the people in the other dorm. When I moved back two weeks later, she seemed passive with me. We talked about it months later, and she told me she was upset that I just left her.

Later that fall semester, the initial spark between Myra and me faded somewhat, but one night in October, we decided to be spontaneous and went to a nearby Pakistani restaurant. We talked over food, then cycled around Madison and walked back to our dorm. It was one of those last warm days before winter set in.

Around the same time, she started seeing this guy, let’s call him Karan. I could never tell if Karan was actually nice. From whatever Myra told me about him, I didn’t like him. Well, I also don’t like most men, but I really didn’t like him.

On the night of Halloween, I was still talking to one of my exes, the same guy who told me that I might have a crush on her. Well, we were just talking. We weren’t in a relationship anymore, but it was something. I don’t know why, but I asked him if I could kiss my friends at the Halloween party, and he was like of course, well, we weren’t seeing each other, of course. That night, I kissed five of my friends. One of the people that I kissed was Myra. At that point, I thought she was the best kisser ever, but I’d say the second best now. She always said that I was her second-best kiss, first being Karan, then. Offended that a man was better than me, anyway. Well, after I told my ex I’d kissed five people, he got upset, and we stopped talking.

So the first week after the breakup was bad because daylight saving time ended at the same time. So the sun started setting earlier. It was hard to get out of bed, and Myra, too, was going through her own thing, so we both would stay in a lot. The week after that, I found comfort in a friend, and we started seeing each other. So the month after that, I spent a lot of time with him. Myra didn’t like that. She didn’t like his name because it reminded her of another person she didn’t like. She didn’t like him at all. So we spent most of the time at his dorm, and I was again barely in my dorm. Then, after a month, I quickly realised I didn’t like him and that I was still not over my ex. We started seeing each other because we were both getting over people, but he caught feelings for me, and I didn’t, so I ended it with him. Then the first thing I do is go tell Myra at Karan’s dorm, and she gets so ecstatic that she hugs me tight.

But things happen; as roommates, we didn’t get along, and to preserve the friendship, I requested a dorm swap. Then comes winter break. I forgave her, and things are fine. We always had a baseline flirty dynamic. She’d make jokes about wanting to kiss me and date me, and I’d laugh them off. But sometimes, in certain moments, we’d giggle and kiss anyway.

After we were back from winter break, we went ice skating, and we thought, oh, it’d be so fun if we put up a picture of us kissing on a Valentine’s wreath in our dorm, so we did exactly that. We kissed again while ice skating, and our friends took a photo while it was snowing.

But then, I moved out of the dorm.

I realised as I left the dorm that I liked her. But I also knew that I didn’t want to do anything about it. So I never told her then. Just started making more of an effort to see her. My dorm was now where my other friends stayed. But I’d still come to see her as much as I could, probably more than I did when I stayed in the dorm.

So two months later, I went as my best friend’s date for his fraternity formal. You know the conversation you have with your best friends at a sleepover before actually falling asleep, it was one of those, right before falling asleep, halfway dozing off, I spilled to him that I like Myra, and told him to keep it a secret, and he did.

After returning from the fraternity formal, whenever I would hang out with him in his dorm, he would keep teasing me with Myra.

Myra started noticing that I had feelings for her, so she kept flirting with me for fun, and eventually, I admitted it to her. But, sadly, after I admitted it, she told me she only liked me platonically, not romantically, but she’d be fine with us just hooking up, casually.

At that moment, I couldn’t just casually hook up with someone that I liked, so I told her that.

Well, we still kept seeing each other. I’d go meet her often. We even started a tradition of Wine Wednesdays because I didn’t have classes on Thursdays, and it’ll be just the two of us, just playing games and sharing wine.

At the same time, I was also dating around a lot. Most of those dates turned out to be really romantic, but I would overshare on first dates and then not want to go on second dates, because I felt a bit too vulnerable. I would come back from those dates and talk to Myra about it, and I could tell from her tone that she was a little jealous. She was still seeing Karan at this point, too.

Then one night at a bar, after a little too many of those “Adult Capri Suns”, we made out. Unfortunately, it was at a bar, and, men being men, they recorded us, which made it really uncomfortable. So we walked back together. I had my arms around her waist, and she was leaning into me. I dropped her off and went back to my dorm.

Then, on a Saturday Night, we were just at her dorm, talking, and then I jokishly sat on her lap, and we were listening to music, one thing led to another, I told her, I liked her, she didn’t say it back, but we started making out, and then played, “Beedi Jalaile,” immediately as the song played, I had to take a moment and realise that this was Myra, and then we stopped making out, and we fell asleep.

Myra got really into stick and poke tattoos, and our song was “Cherry” by Fletcher and Hayley Kiyoko, because she was thinking of getting herself cherry LED lights, and before she bought them, I thought she’d like them and bought them for her. A perfect coincidence. So I let her tattoo a cherry on me.

Then, as finals finished, there were three really hot days in Madison, and after a really cold winter, those hot days felt magical. We would all stay out late, sit on a hill, and talk. I had this obsession with going up the stairs near the hill, by the Abraham Lincoln statue, on the hill, and that’s where Myra and I slow-danced. She knew salsa, so she tried teaching me, but of course, I was really bad at it.

The next day, after hanging out with our friends again, Myra and I went back to our dorms, then called at 12 am and talked on video for the next 3 hours. The call started with us planning hypothetical dates. Myra was still seeing Karan, and she hadn’t even told me she liked me, so I realised in that moment that it wouldn’t work out between us. So I told her that.

It wasn’t until three years later that I found out from my friends that, that same night we slow-danced, she told them she liked me.

After summer break, I tried to distance myself, realizing things wouldn’t work out. She noticed, and things got cold between us. Neither of us could express how we truly felt, leaving much unsaid.

Then months later, we talked and cried because we missed each other. Because, through that year, we had become really close as friends, but at the same time, I was in a relationship, so it would be unfair. We didn’t talk about it after that night.

To this day, we still talk occasionally, even though things didn’t work out. We shared a lot of sweet moments, some romantic and some of a close friendship, and I know she’d appreciate this story. I hope you are doing well.
