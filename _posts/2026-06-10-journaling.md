<span class="day-label">June 10th, 2026</span>
I’ve settled into the routine for '100 days of Making' and will add the amitriptyline marker. Still, I wonder what all this is for. It’s interesting, but I question what’s wrong with me. Why am I like this? I feel secure in myself, but sometimes, all of this—the project, the memories, and the data analysis—makes me feel obsessive. Maybe I was better off before I joined this program and became so fixated on my work. The anxiety is still all-consuming, like before, and in my head. Now I’m processing everything that I went through growing up, which was a lot, and I’m barely picking up the pieces. Most of growing up was alone, and I’ve gotten myself out of my lowest lows by myself. As I said, I don’t know the outcome of this project, but I should finish it. It’s already helped me uncover so much of my childhood that I didn’t realise, admit to myself or even recognise. Maybe it will tell me something more about me. I’m, for the first time, in a space where I feel safe and brave enough to examine all of this and grow from this, actually look into my core. What an odd day to start this.
