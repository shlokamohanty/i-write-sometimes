Pain of my phantom limb.

Priced possession of sorts, never thought you’d be so dear to me.

But it’s not you or what you’re worth, and how hard it might be to get you.

But what you unlocked in me



That I forgot to eat proper food for days, that my clothes were in a pile on the bedroom floor, that I forgot about time, because of some drive. 

I used to look at people with dreams and akin that to delusion, so I made a promise.

Broke that, and here I am, one year later, fallen a-trap to that same drive

I’ll never make it, I’ll never make it.



Should I go back? 

I can hear the whispers, calling.

So,

Maybe I should just become a data analyst. 

Maybe I should remove my piercings.

Maybe I should succumb to heterosexuality,

What the fuck is being non-binary anyway?

Maybe I should marry a mediocre man who dreams of bills, travel, an early 
retirement, and simplicity.

I’ll despise his hands on my body.

But it's fine, I’ll carry our kids.

I’ll swallow my passions and everything about myself. 

and to another, a family, maybe, is the dream.

It’s aching, my phantom limb.



But I’d rather live, I’ll keep trying, whether I make it. 

The sky calls for me to lie on it. 

I’m living, my head up in the clouds, it grounds me.

I’m tethered to the skies. 

Maybe I’ll find someone who’s up there, just like me.

Or is it lonely up there? I’ll go there scared and alone. But I’ll go.

I can’t go back and lie on the grass.

Let me shape the clouds, my life is now in the making.
