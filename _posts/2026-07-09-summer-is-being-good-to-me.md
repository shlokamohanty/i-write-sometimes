I think about the summer a year ago. At this point, in July, I was in India, really sick. Something I kept in mind was that it’ll probably be the last time I'll be back in a while. At this point, in July, I'd met everyone and said my goodbyes. I had found out Apoorv was cheating when I had come back but didn't do anything. We hiked to a waterfall in Asheville, and the night before that, I had told him we should end it. It was the day of our three years. August 21st, I left Charleston. Summer was rather rough. But leaving India, leaving Charleston, both felt like I was leaving behind a past that hurt. I think 2024-2025 was really rough. I lost bits of me in the whole process. I forgot myself there.

ITP happened. I lost myself. I found myself. It’s a mix of who I was and who I am, now. It’s July 2025. I keep going back and forth whether it’s my place or not, but it is what gave me life back. And I like it here. I like the people. I like who I’m becoming. And after a while I actually want to live, simultaneously being much less scared of death. Raviya told me today, I seemed to have had a happy day. I don’t know if I believe in happy anymore, though. I think oblivion leads to happiness now, and I'm not oblivious. But I'm learning to have more fun, find more whimsy in things. I am getting better with my anxiety. It helps knowing that everything is uncertain. I’m trying to succumb myself to the chaos and the disorder that life actually is. Something I never thought would happen, but it’s actually happening. Yesterday, I had blue-pineapple juice. I’ve taken the time out to finally get in touch with people from undergrad because it was hard to do so after the breakup. I speak differently around them. I’m over a lot of my childhood trauma. And that’s a big one. Summer, you have been good to me.
