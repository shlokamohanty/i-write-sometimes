For one of my larger projects, “A letter to you, from the sun”, I started by writing four epistolary poems. Then, for a class assignment, I experimented with grammar-based rules, word choice and synonyms to generate these poems, which was really fun to play with. Letters have become an easy way for me to be more authentic, and vulnerable, so I chose to work with epistolary poems.

While this was really fun to work with, what I struggle with is always the amount of content. If I had more content, the letters wouldn’t be all that predictable. But now, since I chose limited words and sentence structures, the poems are still predictable, and there are certain limitations with the ‘Tracery’ library. However, since this is a long-term project, I will continue working on it over the coming month.
