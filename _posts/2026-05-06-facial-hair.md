Shloka, you’ve not shaved in a while. You have sideburns and a moustache growing, and your eyebrows are going haywire. 

![me-and-facial-hair-1](/i-write-sometimes/assets/images/shloka_hair_1.jpg)

Okay, I’ll shave today. 

It’s a weird feeling today. How quickly hair grows, and consumes, the face, and the scalp. But I suppose hair plays a big role in people’s lives. If I were a strand, perhaps, I’d find myself in knots and entanglements with many now. But, the most entangled to mine, soon slip away. Now, there were two that were knotted, entangled, intertwined, in love, if I may, I love and loved a total of twos. I’ve lost count. I’ve lost track of the entanglements, of the hair that grows on me, outward, inward even.

![me-and-facial-hair-2](/i-write-sometimes/assets/images/shloka_hair_2.jpg)

Now, today, it's all on me, I’ve barely had a strand alone existing, a singular strand. People brush their hair to keep the strands from being entangled with one another. That keeps the hair healthy, too. The most knotted strands always fall off when I brush hard, so perhaps I should brush often. Now, at 23, that’s a scary feeling, realising how little time you spent brushing your hair, and letting knots take precedence over spending time brushing.

![me-and-facial-hair-3](/i-write-sometimes/assets/images/shloka_hair_3.jpg)

But brushing is rather nice, it’s calming, I realise now. It lets my mind wander, making way through the hair to think. Let the more bushy hair flow. Mermaid curls, if I may.

But the entanglements are fun, they are cute, they give me the curls that I like. Maybe I need to brush more often. Time and time again.

23 is weird. 

I shaved my facial hair today.
