Today, I think about ‘I think’ in sentences. Last week, when I was in school, Ryan said, ‘Good, I think.’ I asked him why: ‘I think.’ Then I also thought about how Amisha uses ‘I think’ a lot, especially when I went to visit them in Boston. When I used to talk more with arjun in the first semester, he also used to say, ‘I think’ in sentences a lot. And I’ve been thinking about the importance of saying ‘I think,’ especially since I’ve realised I have a hard time pinning down what I'm feeling. Especially the Madison vs New York Shloka conundrum, from being completely apathetic to having a floodgate of emotions and feeling it to a fault because of the sudden surplus of different emotions and their amalgamations. I sometimes don’t get what the exact feeling is like. While writing my ‘black holes’ essay too, I’m realising that I actually have a lot of trouble pinning down my feelings, and sometimes they manifest in physical sensations. Strange, I think. But since I’m unsure about my feelings, I should be more careful with my sentences and say ‘I think’ more, since that’s more honest than pretending to be sure. It’s also accepting that I’m actually uncertain about what I'm feeling rather than forcing certainty into feeling. So I think I'm going to start saying ‘I think’ more.
