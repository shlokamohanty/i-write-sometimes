 <div class="letter-block">π,
 
did i ever  
tell  
you  
that i can’t do  
math?  
i can't remember numbers. i  
struggle to add two and two together. threes turn  
to sixes. terrified of π.  
letters come to me more naturally than numbers. so i like calculus.  
that i start to derive often strive to reach to the smallest change.  
that the neurons in my brain convolve these derivatives in partials  
connect one to another and reach to multiple answers,  
only to pick the worst one in my head.  
so i’m trying to shuffle again. and perhaps the letters then will reach to 

π,
