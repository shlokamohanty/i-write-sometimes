Kahaani toh nayi likhni hai, par kabhi-kabhi sochti hoon yeh wali bhi acchi hai.

Ek kathakaar ki kahaani, jo mujhse shuru hoti hai, par main toh sirf ek kirdaar hoon. The story of an artist awakening.

Kabhi-kabhi jo lafz main Hindi mein sochta hoon, kaash unhe apni zubaan se bol paati. Meri sachayi kya hai?

Darr-darr ke jeeta hoon. Pyaasi hoon pyaar ke liye, par kabhi waisa mehsoos nahi kiya jaisa chahta hoon. Pyaar bhi darr-darr ke kiya. Thoda paagal bhi hoon.

My skin is thin. I feel everything deeply, par bolti nahi. Bas mehsoos karta hoon.

Freaks, weirdos, geeks. I am one of them.

Kathakaar kabse hoon, pata bhi nahi. Kaise karoon? Kaise likhoon? Main kaun hoon?
