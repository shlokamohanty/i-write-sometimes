~~Am I my own experiment?~~

Shiver-me-timbers, a day and a half long breakdown. A long overdue breakdown. I have this feeling. I don't know quite yet. Something in the midst of love and curiosity, valleying at the jack-of-all-trades ridge.

I was thinking today about noble gases. They are chemically aloof and generally inert. Or feel complete, maybe like me. I'd be Xenon. Noble gases mix well physically, thus the ideal gas mixture. Why react when you don't need to? But noble gases do react, under the right conditions, pressure, and with the right elements. Rare. Costly. But it's needed sometimes. It happens. So when I start reacting, things feel unstable. The cost — anxiety.

I keep thinking, what's really my story? Is it in my abuse, is it my childhood, is it in pain? Is it in my anxiety? The kind that turns up in everything, beautiful in the making. Suddenly, I'm forced to confront these reactions. Or it's Xenon.

At the jack-of-all-trades ridge, you find me, Xenon. Hyper-observant. I remember the tiniest, unspecific details about people, places, and things. Because tracking everything prevents any volatile reactions, logging these like a chemist in a lab, in my memory.

But memory and logs are a funny thing. Now standing in front of me — sudden pressure, sudden shifts, newness. Flipping through the pages, time and time again, I need to flip back and forth to study more, for answers to these sudden changes. To study the familiarity and patterns.

Should I stand back and let Xenon be faced with the unexpected, or create conditions and experiment like a scientist? A good chemist does both.
