I was thinking of one of my friends, Teju, and a conversation we had in undergrad. Well, we've had the same conversation multiple times over the years. We were talking about the Ship of Theseus thought experiment then. Megan recently mentioned in undergrad that there were a lot of things that I did, changing stances well on material things, labels rather, that it was hard to keep up sometimes, but now, because I talk to her and share with her a lot more than I did back then, she understands what’s going on in my head better. I didn’t like flowers initially, then I started liking them. Well, I still do think they die easily, so I like dried flowers. At the start of last year, I didn’t like wearing dresses, I think. I like wearing dresses now. I didn’t wear glasses often. Well, granted, I also had to wear glasses because my eye power changed, and I didn’t want to spend on ordering lenses. Now, I have the right lenses. I still haven’t really worn them, though, but I will maybe, from time to time. But I don’t mind glasses. I thought they gave me headaches, but my eye power was just wrong. I believed, for a long time, a misconstrued fact that when you have astigmatism, you shouldn't wear glasses, whereas it’s the opposite. I go dancing salsa sometimes. I started singing more, even though I’m not good at either, and I didn’t want to earlier, but I’m trying more things. Been confronting a lot about myself and my uglier truths. That I’m this, but I’m also this. I think about the fact that these rather material, label changes, like my hair, me wearing specs, my choice on whether to wear a dress, sometimes, I can almost look deeply into these thoughts, they all lead back to similar or the very same base thought that I had back when I was a kid, maybe when I was 10, only insecurities clouded them over time. So I think of the Ship of Theseus thought experiment today. I think of my shifting identity over years, finally feeling more settled in my nuanced sense, and perhaps even the rest of life will just be figuring out more hypocrisies about myself, which is good in some ways. And I think whether I’m the same person as I always was, or if I'm different. Maybe I’m both an entirely different person and the same person, at the same time.
