So now I’ve realised scripting helps!! A gist of what I said:

Hi, I’m Shloka,  
A lot of you here already know me and, I suppose, a lot of my interests.  
But this summer I questioned a lot of my interests.  
I René Descartes-ed myself.  
I learnt that I don’t actually like games. It became easy to force an interaction with games. I learnt that my medium is drawing and writing.  
All of this started last semester,  
When Cody called me sentimental.  
I didn’t tell him then that I was offended. I looked up the definition of sentimentality.  
I wondered why I was offended. He didn’t mean it in an offensive manner; he was appreciating my skill.  
Well, to me it was a conundrum. How I saw myself. My friends in Madison thought I was quite apathetic. And here in New York I'm making work that's either trying to make meaning of my suffering or overly romantic. Where is the nuance? Can I not be a person who can hold two truths? Over the summer, I called it the Madison-New York conundrum. I do notice now that, in fact, I'm a bit of both. Not completely apathetic and not too driven by emotions. Just somewhere in between.  
I do think sentimental work is manipulative; it is an attempt to sway the audience to feel one way. 
So as you guys already know, I studied physics partly in undergrad; I'm fascinated by it. I started reading up on the philosophies of black holes. Maybe going inside a black hole will answer a lot of my questions. That’s impossible, though. The vastness of black holes is scary, but at the same time I’m awestruck. It’s wondrous. And now my interest lies in accepting that it is okay that I won't have the answer to the Theory of Everything.
