Zun had been dating since she was 12. Most of the people she dated were different from each other, but most of their names started with the letter ‘R’, barring perhaps two people. So when Zun first met Rahul, whom she now jokingly calls Ra, she was like, not another person whose name starts with an ‘R’, even though this felt different. At this point, she should get the letter ‘R’ tattooed. Let alone, his name was Rahul. Rahul! A name she and her best friend, Rahima, used often, for so many of their projects, and random things since growing up. So subconsciously, had she again manifested this? The way she manifested dating Rithik. Well, if she gets the letter ‘R’ tattooed and it doesn’t work out, she could just say it is for Rahima.

So the kiss with Rahul. The kiss with him. She kissed him. ‘I kissed Rahul’, she texted her best friend at 2 am while waiting for her bus. Of course, Rahima predicted this. Zun talked about Rahul a bit too much for someone who is only platonic with him. She hadn’t kissed anyone like that. There was one that was ever so slightly similar, but nowhere near as good as this, and her name didn’t start with an ‘R’, anyway. In that moment, she didn’t think of the rats, or that she ended it with Ranak just last week, or that she hadn’t had dinner, or that she had an assignment to finish, or that she was cold. Right before they kissed, she had offered her jacket to him, even though she was freezing. She wasn’t really thinking, for once. She had only ended it with Ranak last month. Is this too quick after a breakup? Is this too soon? She thought incessantly that night as she took the A train, then the G train back home. There was no reason for her to take the A-G train. She just liked the extra 10 min walk from the Broadway station to her house so she could listen to music a little longer. Rahul stayed two stops away from hers. She could have just gone with him.

In July, they started a new job at an architecture firm in DUMBO, and that’s where they met. They were working on the same project team. Together, they were designing a glass building, but the shape would be such that, as the winter sun comes in, the building could retain some of that solar heat. It was Zun’s first job, and she felt like all eyes were on her. She thought there would be more training coming into the new job, but it was more like figuring it out on her own. She had many questions, but she was a little scared and, at times, even embarrassed to ask for help. Rahul, on the other hand, had worked before, so he expected that there wouldn’t be any handholding. He was also confused, but his experiences helped him get a better grip on planning what he needed to do and how he needed to do it. He noticed very early on that Zun was figuring it all out and was really confused. So, he would ask her questions to help her out and prompt her to think. They would go for walks at 6 pm after they finished work around Brooklyn Bridge Park, and after that, they’d part ways at the York St Station. She’d take the A train into Brooklyn, and he’d take the F to the Manhattan side. Even though she knew she could take the same route as him. Oftentimes, they'd eat dinner together at the park on a bench while conversing, which was when he’d ask her questions. It had become their routine for months. Zun would get flustered by his questions. It’ll make her think about things, and she didn’t like that. It felt like someone was tapping into her, trying to get to her, trying to break her walls down, something that Rishit would do often. She and Rishit would also go on these walks and talk, and he’d ask her questions about herself, nudge her, and tell her that he saw her as someone complex and complicated, instead of being by her side when she needed him. Is Rahul like Rishit? Does Rahul also see me as someone who is complicated and complex? Something that would run through her head often. Truth is, whenever someone asked her any questions that made her introspect even slightly about herself, she’d relate them to Rishit. Is Rahul like Rishit?

Rishit and Zun were friends for a while. They dated briefly, then were friends again. He knew the books she would like and would give her those to read. He knew her well and the things she’d like. They were friends, but they’d stargaze, didn’t really kiss ever, but always got close to each other, especially when she put her head on his shoulder while they were talking. But later she found out he’d change his narrative depending on who he was with, giving them contradictory information about himself. Play a different character with each person he meets. Even give her letters which to her seemed so profound, but write the same ones to other people. So she started hating letters. His questions would also make her think and affect good things, and even good people she’d like. In some ways, he was scared of losing her, so he’d just do enough to keep her around. So when she got together with Rithik, she briefly stopped talking to Rishit, even though they had been friends for 5 years, because she knew that he’d say stuff about Rithik that would again make her question.

So, on a cold night in September, they stayed in the park a little longer than usual. Even that day, he asked her questions about her. But she wasn’t getting flustered.

She realised that day that her fear was, in fact, irrational. Everything there was to know about Rahul was out there, and everyone in the office said the same things about him.

But Rahul wasn’t like Rishit.  There was no contradiction. Rahul just saw her as a confused person, not complex and complicated.
