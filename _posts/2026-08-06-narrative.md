My last year of undergrad was very anxiety-ridden because of the question of what's next, wanting and wanting to stay. Next year is coming soon. I can anticipate some anxiety ~~in regards with the uncertainty that it will uphold~~. I need to change the narratives in my brain.

I sometimes need to structure my thoughts and compartmentalise feelings. Otherwise, I quickly feel overwhelmed and unregulated. And when it comes to how to structure my thoughts or follow a methodology, many times I feel self-righteous. ~~I'm a pretty logical person.~~ Sometimes, I have a backup plan, and a backup of that plan. I'm rather egoistic there. ~~There's a good thing that this comes with awareness.~~ Or, maybe that prevents anxiety spirals. This is a narrative of control.

~~I can continue to structure my thoughts and compartmentalise my feelings.~~ When unexpected things happen, I need to be confident that I can handle the situations, because when I was a kid, I probably dealt with things that were statistically worse. I'll follow my methodologies and structures, but I'll also accept things that come to me with slowness and reflection, ~~so I am not self-righteous~~. I'll refrain from analysing in the present moment. ~~Be more tolerant.~~ This is a narrative of uncertainty.

Maybe control and uncertainty are not as much at odds. Maybe structure helps with not knowing what is next. Something survivable.
