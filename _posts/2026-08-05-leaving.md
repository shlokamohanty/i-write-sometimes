I reconnected with one of my best friends, Abhiraam, after two years. Another relationship where I just left quietly. I had three big friend groups during my undergraduate years. The first and the second friend group largely remained the same, some people left, some people came in. Well, again I left, then I made new friends, then some of those friends from the other group joined. Then things became difficult with a few, and I left. I hate that sometimes, leaving feels like second nature, restarting, rather than staying, because growing up, I stayed too long. Leaving quietly is also one of the most painful experiences. I realise now. So I am left with guilt for the relationships I did that to. But talking to Abhiraam made me realise how easy it was for us to reconnect. Some relationships can withstand time and distance.

Abhiraam told me that my ex is actually moving to the UK, and he is really excited about it because he’ll get to be with his family. And I like New York. Thinking about **[goals](https://shlokamohanty.github.io/i-write-sometimes/2025/10/06/goals.html)**.

As I listen to ['bowie box'](https://youtu.be/ylwu0ejbPjU?si=_9973wnjI1AvQQAO), I feel older than I felt when I first moved to New York. The first year after undergrad is over + ITP has been a recipe for extreme existentialism, sadness, grief, escapism, coming to terms with holding my own complexities and of others as well. I have succumbed to uncertainty and chaos. *Even the most deterministic systems cannot be predicted*.

*But I will try to stay more often now. Because I’m not the person anymore who stayed too long*.
