Hmm.

I guess I have been thinking. Been listening to ['Gulabi'](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E4ZJxhyAaH8&list=RDE4ZJxhyAaH8&start_radio=1) a lot.

Used to listen to it a lot freshman year of my undergrad. Teju used to play it a lot in his dorm. I have been missing him lately, one of my best friends. We were talking yesterday about a fight people had in undergrad over a bathroom, causing a massive crack in the wall and a leak. And I found that hilarious, as did he.

I think about freshman year sometimes. The breakup. Then Ishan and I were seeing each other. Then I decided that we should stop, because I was missing my ex then. Then I stopped talking to Ishan for around 4 months. Then Teju made us sit in a room and record a video of us talking to each other after months, because he couldn’t stand two of his friends not talking to each other. Then Ishan and I became friends. Ishan is my ex’s bestfriend’s cousin. Ishan is with Rea now, and Rea and I are really good friends too. She calls me ‘cookie’. I miss Ishan and Rea too.

I was really messy in 2021. Change always makes me messy. Mumbai to Madison. Messy. First year in the US. Messy. But, ’Gulabi’.

Surprisingly, we all remained friends through it.

But it was my first year experiencing seasons. Winter was brutal. Spring was romantic. And I still remember the first summer in the US after winter. It was the most beautiful summer.

But I’d live like this, and then suddenly, things would come crashing down - something small- and an anxiety crash.

After that I didn’t really like summers all that much.

But I do miss when I could fake confidence then. But there was a problem there. After a bit, I realised. The faking confidence. The ability to make anything romantic, then. I realise now that I would make people like this ‘idea of me’ rather than me, actually. I didn’t quite let people in, I think. I do more now. Maybe I know myself better now.

I guess it’s nice knowing that if I have to go back at the end of ITP, I'll still have Teju and Ishan in Mumbai.

I wonder what moving back to Mumbai would be like. It's my sixth year here.

So Madison to New York. I liked this summer. Summer. I needed it. I got sucked into books. I don’t know how many I read. I get flustered by pigeons fluttering. But I feel a lot less anxious now. I notice I can regulate much more quickly; I hop more, more clumsily. It’s nice. Less confident but more myself. Scripting helps. I catch myself saying my weird thoughts out loud, and I'm like, that's weird, but it's true. Amisha and Jatin find my recent revelations hilarious and surprising. I suppose I'm more vulnerable now.

Hmm.
