This framework is for expressive work.

I think drawing, writing, and filming are mediums with direct exposure and need to be handled with care. While I do make for myself, sometimes my work has an audience. This framework will help me learn which work is just for myself and which one is intended for an audience. The exposure and expressiveness of the medium can unintentionally cause harm. There’s a responsibility that I need to take there; hence, the framework.

**Without looking at the structure of the medium, it is actually very easy to fall into the work being extremely expressive.** That’s why some of my work is hard to look at after making, like ‘Shadows by the blue light’ and my ‘Rewiring Connections: the log’.

**I think there’s a difference between reliving while making and for/after processing.** I think this is what arjun meant by distance while making, but I’m not entirely sure. For ‘Shadows by the blue light’, as strange as it sounds, it felt like I had to relive it to make it, which makes looking at it now hard. I wasn’t looking at it from outside.

I think writing letters is that good middle, where I’m vulnerable; so was ‘Rewiring Connections: the project’. **But the nature of the log makes it harder to continue the project, even though it’s a living project, because it became a microblog accidentally. Even though it was really important for me to do the project to process.** But the log was helpful; it made me reflect and question, became evidence that something was off last semester with me.

The black holes essay’s approach is also good, because it is learning about a feeling, an essay about black holes, which also happens to be about me, so at the interpretation level. But the medium makes it quite expressive, in general. So, now I think a layer of abstraction and inquiry comes from looking at the structure of the medium. Sometimes, expressive work is fine; to actually **express**, it can be kept to myself - maybe my means of crying sometimes - the sentimental work. It’s weird sometimes; I have this feeling that I have to make to release. But lately, I haven’t had that; surprisingly, I’m processing my mom’s accident with a lot more care towards myself. I haven’t felt the necessity/urge to draw or write my feelings about it, yet. Maybe I’m processing things differently since summer, rather than it being reactive.

I’m realising why letters become a good medium for me to write with than just journaling; **there’s a boundary, because it is directed towards someone.** I think the structure gives the distance. I think **if I’m able to see the nuance of the situation, and if I am, then I’ve processed.**

I think there are three ways to look at this: making as processing, making for processing, making after processing.
