Going back to the intimidation part. I think I end up projecting what I do, thinking that other people also do that. My anxious brain tries to fit people into models. And, sometimes, I assume the other person is also doing the same. I'm intimidated by people who I think do what I do, which is making conclusions. It's converting these judgements into things that are essentially settled. Truly curious people stick to these assumptions loosely. They allow these models to keep updating. Maybe I'm not as curious as I thought, or maybe it's this anxiety that's stopping me from being curious thoroughly.
